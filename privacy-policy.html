Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ANAY Premium Stone handles information received through its website, enquiry channels and Meta lead forms.

Last Updated: 29 September 2026

1. Introduction

ANAY Premium Stone ("ANAY Premium Stone", "APS", "we", "us" or "our") respects the privacy of people who contact us through our website, enquiry forms, social media channels and other business communication channels.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may receive, how we may use it, when it may be shared, and how you can contact us about your information.

2. Company / Business Identification

Business Name: ANAY Premium Stone (APS)

ANAY Premium Stone is a natural stone and sandstone quarry owner, manufacturer, processor, supplier, bulk supplier and exporter based in Sarmathura, Dholpur, Rajasthan, India.

Our website provides information about Rajasthan sandstone products, product formats, applications, manufacturing capabilities and business enquiries for domestic and international buyers.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you contact us and what you request, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your company or business name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your phone number or WhatsApp number.
  • Your business or professional information.
  • Your sandstone or natural stone product requirements.
  • Required quantity, volume or approximate order requirements.
  • Project details, application details or other enquiry information you choose to provide.
  • Information submitted through forms on our website.
  • Information submitted through Facebook, Instagram or other Meta lead forms.
  • Basic website technical information, such as browser, device, IP address and website usage information, where applicable through website technologies or analytics services.

You should only provide information that is relevant to your enquiry and that you are authorised to share.

4. How We Use Information

We may use the information we receive for legitimate business and communication purposes, including to:

  • Respond to product, quotation and business enquiries.
  • Understand sandstone specifications and customer requirements.
  • Prepare quotations and discuss pricing, quantities, specifications and supply requirements.
  • Discuss sandstone products, samples, production and export requirements.
  • Contact potential buyers regarding the enquiry they submitted.
  • Provide product information, technical information and export information.
  • Process and manage business enquiries.
  • Follow up on enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Improve our website, communication and services.

If you submit your contact details for a product or business enquiry, ANAY Premium Stone may use those details to contact you regarding your enquiry, sandstone products, quotations and bulk sandstone supply.

5. How We Protect Information

We take reasonable practical steps to protect information received through our website and business enquiry channels against unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore do not represent that information transmitted over the internet is completely risk-free.

6. When Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared or made available where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

  • Service providers, website providers, technology providers or business service providers that help us operate our website, communication or enquiry processes.
  • Advertising and lead-generation platforms, including Meta, where applicable to a lead or enquiry submitted through Facebook or Instagram.
  • Government authorities, regulators, courts or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Where information is shared with a service provider, we seek to use it only for the relevant business or technical purpose.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, analytics or other website-related purposes.

The ANAY Premium Stone website uses Google Analytics to understand website usage and improve the website. This may involve the collection of technical and usage information, such as browser, device and interaction information, through cookies or similar technologies.

Your browser may allow you to control or block cookies. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website functionality or analytics.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may use or link to third-party services, platforms or websites. These may include services used for website analytics, social media, advertising, communication or other business functions.

Third-party websites and services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. ANAY Premium Stone is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that you choose to visit.

9. Facebook, Instagram and Meta Lead Forms

ANAY Premium Stone may use Meta advertising services, including Facebook and Instagram lead forms, to receive business enquiries.

If you submit your information through a Meta lead form, the information you provide may be made available to ANAY Premium Stone for the purpose described in the form and for handling the resulting business enquiry.

We may use information received through Meta lead forms to contact you regarding the enquiry you submitted, sandstone products, quotations, specifications, quantities, project requirements and bulk supply.

Meta may process information under its own terms and privacy policies. You should review the applicable Meta privacy information for details about how Meta handles information on its platforms.

10. Data Retention

We may retain enquiry and business contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, prepare and manage quotations, maintain business communication, provide requested services, maintain business records, or meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the purpose for which it was received.

11. User Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the information involved, you may have the right to request information about the personal information we hold about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, or request deletion of information where appropriate.

You may also choose not to provide information requested through an enquiry form. However, some information may be necessary for us to respond to a quotation or business enquiry.

12. Requests for Correction or Deletion

If you believe that information we hold about you is inaccurate, or if you want to request correction or deletion of your information, please contact ANAY Premium Stone using the contact details below.

Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant enquiry or records. We may need to verify the request before taking action. Certain information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, legal or regulatory reasons.

13. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business and professional enquiries relating to natural stone and sandstone products. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the information and take appropriate action where applicable.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business processes or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be revised.

15. Contact Information

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, want to request correction or deletion of information, or have a privacy-related enquiry, please contact ANAY Premium Stone.
