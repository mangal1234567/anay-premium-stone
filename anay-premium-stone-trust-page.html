Section 02 — Company

Company Overview

ANAY Premium Stone is an Indian sandstone manufacturing and supply company based in Rajasthan, India. The company manufactures Dholpur Beige Sandstone, Dholpur Red Sandstone and Bansi Paharpur Pink Sandstone while also supplying additional Indian sandstone varieties through trusted manufacturing partners.

The company supplies architects, builders, contractors, distributors, importers, wholesalers and project developers across domestic and international markets.

Section 04 — Products & Supply Network

Products & Supply Network

Core manufactured products, backed by a wider sourcing network for additional varieties.

Manufactured In-House

Dholpur Beige Sandstone

A fine-grained beige sandstone quarried in the Dholpur region of Rajasthan, valued for its uniform tone and workability in paving, cladding and flooring applications.

OriginDholpur, Rajasthan
Common usePaving · Flooring · Cladding
Manufactured In-House

Dholpur Red Sandstone

A warm red-toned sandstone from the same Dholpur belt, used widely in facades, boundary work and traditional architectural detailing.

OriginDholpur, Rajasthan
Common useFacades · Boundary walls · Detailing
Manufactured In-House

Bansi Paharpur Pink Sandstone

A dense pink sandstone from Bansi Paharpur, historically used in monumental construction across northern India, prized for durability and finish quality.

OriginBansi Paharpur, Rajasthan
Common useMonumental & heritage-style work · Cladding
Additional Varieties. Beyond its own manufactured range, ANAY Premium Stone sources further Indian sandstone varieties — including other regional colours, finishes and formats — through a network of trusted manufacturing partners, matched to individual customer requirements.
